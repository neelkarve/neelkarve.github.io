# About me

## Bio
Neel graduated from Yale-NUS College in 2024 with a Bachelor of Science in Physical Sciences, and is a strong proponent of an interdisciplinary university education. He is passionate about enabling a just and equitable clean energy transition, and is now a consultant at a specialist energy transition advisory firm. He hopes to bring lessons from China's renewable energy development back home to Southeast Asia. After hours, Neel is a volunteer bicycle mechanic, avid cyclist, and guitarist. Neel is from Singapore.
